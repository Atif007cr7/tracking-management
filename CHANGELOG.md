# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (beta) — 25 Oct 2026

First public beta, macOS only.

- Screen-time tracking per app, with idle, sleep and screen-lock detection.
- 3D companion docked to the screen edge or the MacBook notch, with speech bubbles.
- Panel with Today, Apps, Goals and Subscriptions tabs; light, dark and system themes.
- Dashboard with daily chart, focus mix, app list and companion picker.
- Google sign-in (invited testers only during the beta).
- Optional Gmail receipt scanning for subscription renewals.
